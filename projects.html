Research & Projects

I like working where theory meets messy reality — where models run into uncertainty, and technical methods (occasionally) help make sense of complex systems.

Evaluating LDA and QDA with Conformal Prediction & Calibration

Master Thesis, Imperial College London (2025)

My current research explores uncertainty quantification in classification models. I’m investigating how classic tools like LDA and QDA perform when judged not just on accuracy, but on trustworthiness — using conformal prediction, proper scoring rules, and calibration methods to evaluate sharpness and reliability in probabilistic forecasts. A key part of the project applies these techniques to political forecasting: when models predict elections, how much should we actually believe them?

YOLO for Hurricane Damage Detection

EY Open Data Science Challenge (2024) — Top 5% Team

Built a computer vision model using YOLO to identify hurricane impact zones in Puerto Rico from satellite images. The project focused on improving disaster response by enabling faster, automated damage assessment — bridging machine learning with real-world crisis management.

Quantum Economics — Making New Ideas Practical

Talks, Research & Projects (2023–2025)

Exploring how quantum computing might reshape optimization problems in economics and finance. From portfolio optimization to market design, I’ve worked on translating abstract quantum models into ideas that make sense outside the lab — organizing talks, building toy models, and presenting at student research events to make quantum economics a little less intimidating.
