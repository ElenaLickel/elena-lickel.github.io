About Me

I am driven by a deep curiosity for how complex systems work — and how we can model, optimize, and predict their behavior. My academic path has taken me through business analytics, mathematics, and computer science, and I am now focused on the intersection of quantum computing and economics. Currently preparing for a PhD, I am especially interested in using computational methods — from agent-based modeling to quantum algorithms — to understand and solve real-world decision problems. Whether it is building interpretable models or exploring new optimization techniques, I am most engaged when theory meets impactful application.
